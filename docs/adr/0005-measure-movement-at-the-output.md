# Measure movement at the output, not inside the loop

Stage 6 Modulation's Decorrelation invariant is about Channels — per-Channel LFOs must not move together — but the `all-v1` Stage capture profile records only `00-split.wav` and `01-diffusion-step-{i}.wav`. There is no Feedback Loop capture boundary, and the Feedback Loop is Modulation's default and most consequential target, so the N-Channel claim cannot be measured where it matters without adding one. We decided not to add it: `modulation-v1` measures **Output correlation** between the two Downmix outputs and coherent pitch movement in the summed output, and the per-Channel claim is asserted structurally at resolution instead — every Channel's LFO phase, rate spread, and selection are drawn from distinct positional seeds (`FLOOPMPH`/`FLOOPMRT`/`FLOOPMCH`, `DSTEPMPH`/`DSTEPMRT`/`DSTEPMCH`), so decorrelated LFOs are true by construction rather than by observation.

This is a deliberate deviation from this project's usual instinct, and from ADR-0003's own reasoning that internal Stage evidence is worth capturing: everywhere else, an internal claim gets an internal capture. The trade-off accepted here is measurement fidelity at the default target, against versioning a capture profile that is a published contract — `all-v1` would have had to become `all-v2`, with a new boundary, new manifest entries, and its own equivalence story, to verify a property that the seeding already guarantees. What the output can still answer is the question the stage document actually asks in its invariant ("no coherent frequency modulation at the LFO rate **in the summed output**"), and what it cannot answer is the internal mechanism behind `channelFraction` — whether the mixing matrix redistributes detuning, and how fast — which is therefore recorded as an output-level hypothesis, evaluated by metric convergence across the `channel-fraction` sweep axis rather than by direct observation.

The naming follows: measuring L and R means measuring Downmix outputs, which are explicitly not Channels (see CONTEXT.md), so the metric is **Output correlation** and never "channel decorrelation". Reversing this decision later means adding a Feedback Loop capture boundary in a new capture profile version and extending `modulation-v1`; nothing in the current design forecloses that.
